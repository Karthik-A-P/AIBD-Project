  Setup
  - It loads Final_Dataset_AIBD.csv and drops rows with no ICU label. That
    leaves 100 samples, 50 ICU and 50 non-ICU, with 17,724 features after
    encoding Gender.
  - The data is split 75/25, stratified by class: 75 samples for training and 25
    for testing.
  - It then normalizes with StandardScaler, fit on the training set only so no
    test information leaks in.
